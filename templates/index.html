01 / TASK

Detect a condition, then inspect its evidence.

The detector identifies current station disturbances from available readings and the assembly graph. Its training target is simulator disturbance severity of at least 0.30.

An alert is a request to review the station. It does not establish a physical failure or a causal diagnosis.

Observed readings + sensor mask + graph → Detection score
02 / DATA

A controlled simulation environment.

Forty stations connect body construction, paint shop and final assembly. Shift scenarios vary the location, timing, severity and combination of injected disturbances.

Missing readings stay unavailable. Simulator answers and hidden sensor readings are excluded from model inputs and operational status.

03 / EVALUATION

Separate complete shifts.

128 shifts train the model. 32 validation shifts select the checkpoint and detection threshold. Another 32 shifts supply the original held-out results.

Additional robustness conditions evaluate the frozen model. The model, training scales and threshold are not adjusted during these evaluations.

04 / SCOPE

Clear boundaries for each capability.

All measured results use the same synthetic generator and graph. Real-factory performance and financial savings have not been established.
